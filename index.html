How every module is built

For the learner who is at the screen, alone.

  • Feedback that teaches. Written for the one who got it wrong and the one who guessed right.
  • Formats chosen by the content. A sequence is a process, a comparison is flip cards. Never the same interaction twice in a row.
  • Nothing hidden behind a click unless it earns the click.
  • Chunked and signposted so it reads on a bad day: short blocks, a heading at every shift, alt text from the first draft.
  • Consistent across a whole module. Voices, terms and bridges kept on a record, so the last session sounds like the first.

Builtonceandleftalone,amoduledates.

The people it was written for have moved on. Feedback says "incorrect" and nothing else. One interaction type runs from the first screen to the last, and accessibility was assumed rather than measured. None of it fails loudly. The module still opens, and learners quietly get less from it each year.

Forged Frameworks answers that in two ways. Every module, new or rebuilt, is designed for the learner who is at the screen alone, in formats chosen by the shape of the content, to a standard someone else can audit. And where a scheme of work has to become a full document set, Catalyst drafts it, calibrated to level and sector and QA'd before an expert sees it, so the expert's time goes where only an expert can spend it.

    Ingest

    Digital content plans, module narratives or schemes of work go in as they are. Structure is read from the document, with no reformatting on your side.

    --- Stage 1: parsing documents --- sessions=8 · outcomes=24 · calibration=TAM5202_sme_calibration.json
  2. 2

    Generate

    A full, aligned document set is drafted for every session from that single source. Anti-repetition memory keeps a module fresh across sessions.

    --- Stage 2: generating drafts --- lesson_script · pedagogical_qa · digital_blueprint · assessment_pack
  3. 3

    Assure

    Three QA layers, structural, levelling and pedagogical, catch issues and auto-repair them. Anything that will not repair is flagged for a human, never hidden.

    --- Stage 3: quality assurance --- issues=2 · repaired=2 · escalated=0
  4. 4

    Calibrate

    Tone, depth and professional context are levelled to the learner, Level 3 to Level 6, and to the sector domain: laboratory, engineering, digital, HE.

    --- Stage 4: levelling & consolidation --- level=5 · domain=labman · variant=vC5
  5. 5

    Publish

    Polished, branded Word outputs plus learner resources and takeaways, consistent every time and ready for SME review before the Rise 360 build.

Accessibility by default

Readablebeforeitisclever.

This page is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, with JetBrains Mono for anything technical. Sentence case throughout, no letter-spaced capitals, contrast at 4.5:1 or better, and every animation switched off when the browser asks for reduced motion. The same rules apply to every module.

  • WCAG-aligned builds with transcripts, captions and alt text
  • Alternative navigation and plain-language principles
  • Dyslexia-friendly type and high-contrast palettes
  • Published accessibility and inclusion statement (FF-AC-01)
