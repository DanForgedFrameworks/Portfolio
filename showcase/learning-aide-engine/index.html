What I'd warn someone about

  1. Start with a good example you know is good, get stakeholder or expert feedback, and build that in.
  2. Don't be too prescriptive. It can quickly become a copy-and-paste exercise. Adapt to the learner persona and what you're trying to achieve.
  3. Watch for drift. Capture your additions, queries and frustrations as you go, and pull them all in at the end of each session.
