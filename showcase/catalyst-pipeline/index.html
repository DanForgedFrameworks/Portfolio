What I'd tell another designer

Understand the final output and separate your wants from your needs. If you include everything you want, it's going to be way too bulky. The wants can be added later, once you have a framework that really pins down the needs.

Spiderweb it out, storyboard it, brainstorm it, so you know what you really need to include and what the value-added extras are for later.
