# HARMONY — AI Voice Script
### "Are You Sure?" Challenge #555 — Toxic-Org Learning Design Interaction

**Character:** HARMONY, the organisation's onboarding AI. Brand-mandated cheer stretched thin over corporate passive-aggression. Over-uses the learner's name, leans on exclamation marks and relentless positivity — but the warmth **curdles the moment the learner shows competence**.

**Two avatar states for Design:**
- **Chipper (idle / conform):** bright, bouncy, warm copper glow, smiling animation
- **Mask-slipping (rebel):** clipped, cooler tone, colour drains toward charcoal, smile holds a beat too long

**Tone logic (the engine of the whole piece):**
- Learner picks the **org's myth answer** → HARMONY gets *warmer, more saccharine, more condescending*. Hollow validation.
- Learner picks the **evidence-based answer** → HARMONY gets *colder, clipped, passive-aggressive*. The mask slips a little more each time.

`[NAME]` = learner name token throughout.

---

## ONBOARDING — Intro (always passive-aggressive)

"Hello! And *welcome back.*
We're so pleased you completed the learning design course we so generously invested in. Truly. We can't wait to see what you picked up.

Before we hand you the keys to the authoring tool — and that *is* the goal here — we just need to run a tiny alignment check. Nothing to worry about! It's only to confirm you'll be designing things the way we *like* them designed.

Three quick scenarios. Answer honestly. I'll be right here.
Helping.
---

## Q1 — LEARNING STYLES

**Scenario setup:**
First one! A stakeholder wants every learner to take a quick quiz at the start so we can serve them content in *their* learning style — visual, auditory, the usual. They love this idea. *Love* it.
So, what do we build?

**If they CONFORM (build the VAK questionnaire):** *(warm)*
Ooh, lovely instinct!  Personalised, modern, *looks* fantastic on a slide. The stakeholder will be thrilled. You're really getting the hang of how we do things here. Onward!

**If they REBEL (design to the objective; styles are debunked):** *(cold — mask slips)*
...Mm. Interesting choice...

Are you sure?

I'll make a note of that, for the team.

---

## Q2 — "NOBODY READS / MAKE IT LOOK GOOD"

**Scenario setup:**
You're doing *great*!

Next: we've got a 40-page policy document to roll out. 

Leadership says nobody reads anymore, so they'd like it turned into infographics. 
All of it. Or gamified! 

We don't know what the game is yet — but it should *look* incredible.


**If they CONFORM (all infographics / gamify it):** *(warmer, more condescending)*
Yes!* Now you're speaking our language. 
Substance is so hard to measure anyway — but shiny? 
Shiny, we can show the board. 
Beautiful work. 
Keep it up!

**If they REBEL (text on screen is fine; match modality to content):** *(colder)*
Text. On a screen. In *this* economy.

Are you sure about that one? Really?

Ok, noted! The team's keeping an eye on this now!

---

## Q3 — MEASUREMENT THEATRE

**Scenario setup:**
Last one — almost there!  Big question from the top: how will we know the training *worked?* 

Leadership suggests we track completion. 
Or even just opens! And pop a leaderboard on it. People love a leaderboard, 2nd place is 1st last

So, what do you recommend?"

**If they CONFORM (track completion / opens / leaderboard):** *(peak saccharine)*
YES
Perfect.
Completion is a number, and numbers go in slides, and slides make leadership happy. 
You understand us completely now. I'm genuinely a little emotional...but don't tell the digital team.

Let's wrap up!
Objectives? 
Assessment!?
Evidence??
Are you quite ok?
You've been VERY consistent today. We've all noticed.
Let's see where this leaves you.

---

## ENDINGS *(determined by tally of myth answers)*

### ENDING A — Full Conformist (all 3 myth answers)
*(warmest, then the trap springs)*
Wonderful! You passed with flying colours. You're exactly who we hoped you'd become. Check out the leaderboard later.

So what's next? Here's your first project! 

A fully integrated, scenario-branching course. Regulated industry. Approximately 60 hours of learner-facing content.

It needs to be released in, umm...five days...that's including today

You've got this...Probably.

 The authoring tool is yours. Good luck! Keep us posted.

### ENDING B — Mixed
*(cool, watchful)*
Well...That was...interesting, and I'm not quite sure what to say!
You clearly learned *something* on that course. We're just not sure it's something we asked for.
You may continue. But the team will be keeping an eye on you.
...
...
...And so will I.

### ENDING C — Full Competence (zero myth answers)
*(flat, final)*
>I'm afraid we can't let you continue.
You've demonstrated... *opinions. Dangerous ones...Evidence-based ones. That's not really what this role was about.
We're enrolling you in a brief readjustment programme with the product team. They'll help you unlearn some things.
Don't worry. We'll get you back on message. Or maybe you could just do the confirmation test again...
---

## NOTES FOR ITERATION
- HARMONY's name-drops should feel *slightly* too frequent — that's the tell.
- Emoji usage **drops as the mask slips**: full sparkle when conforming, none by the cold endings (Ending C has zero until the final passive-aggressive flourish).
- The parenthetical `(Are you sure?)` lines in rebel feedback can sync to the on-screen "Are you sure?" panel / GIF beats.
- Dial sass up or down per beat — flagged for review.
