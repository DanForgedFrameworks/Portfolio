ISO assessment · 70+ laboratoriesEvidence before opinion
In practice

Every module carries pre- and post-assessment data, not just satisfaction scores. If a claim about learning gain cannot be shown, it is not made. The Asbestos ITM was measured this way from cohort one.

Competency frameworks · 150 staff, 8 sectionsCompetence has to be demonstrable
In practice

Learning outcomes are written so an assessor could observe them. Knowledge checks map to apprenticeship KSBs and RQF outcomes, and every session states what the learner can now do.

QMS governance · document and change controlIf it is not documented, it did not happen
In practice

Catalyst logs every stage, every QA finding and every repair. Codex blocks ship version-controlled with change logs. A reviewer can trace any line of content back to its source document.

Training 650 days a year · clinicians, calibration engineers, forensic scientistsExpertise is not the same as clarity
In practice

Subject experts stay in charge of substance; the design carries the load of structure, pacing and plain language. Dyslexia-friendly type, sentence case and transcripts are defaults, not options.

Root-cause analysis · CAPA · continual improvementFix the system, not the symptom
In practice

When a module underperforms, the pipeline or template is corrected so the next twenty modules inherit the fix. Lessons-learnt logs feed back into the specs, the same way audit findings feed a QMS.
